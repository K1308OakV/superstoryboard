Production Settei Packet

Animation-use character sheets for the two scene-critical designs. These pages are not moodboards; they define one coherent character per sheet, the required views, material rules, and the way each design should survive camera moves, lighting changes, and later 3D blocking.

Animation Reference

Silent Armored Knight

V7 Settei Pass
  • Final design goal: one pitch-black, mobile, fear-inducing dark knight silhouette with moderate cowl fins, floor-length cape, tactical armor, and readable full-body proportions.
  • Use for animation: maintain armor plate layout across front, side, back, and closeup. The cape can add threat, but must not hide the action silhouette.
  • Optics rule: inactive in first reveal; white-blue tactical optics activate only later as a controlled escalation.

Batman Sheet Requirements

For Next Render

Turnaround

Front, three-quarter, side, back, and one compact action stance. Same armor geometry must be preserved in every view.

Cowl

Engineered face guard, moderate fins, thick neck seal, no exposed face. The eyes are physical slits, not magical glow.

Materials

Near-black graphite ceramic plates, flexible black undersuit, thin wet edge highlights, scratch hatching, no gray-knight read.

Movement

Built for sprinting, pivoting, grappling, and fast strikes. Armor is sturdy, but not bulky or statue-like.

Female Two-Face

V7 Settei Pass
  • Final design goal: one coherent mature copper-blonde formal matriarch with a composed good side and a severe stylized damaged side. V3 remains the face identity anchor; V7 improves the animation turnaround structure.
  • Use for animation: V7 gives usable front, side, back, walking pose, coat silhouette, and expression strip references. The exact half-face reveal still needs to preserve V3's approved good-side identity.
  • Damage rule: harden the damaged side through charred texture, hair loss on that side, shadow, neck/arm continuity, and torn formalwear. Keep it stylized and not medical.

Two-Face Sheet Requirements

For Next Render

Turnaround

Concealed front, revealed front, side hiding damaged side, three-quarter reveal, back view, and walking pose with frame.

Face

Good half keeps the approved V3 identity: severe eyes, copper-blonde hair, grief held under control, not glamour-soft.

Reveal

Damaged half must read instantly in flicker light: absent hair on damaged side, charred leathery surface, scar plates, sunken shadow.

Wardrobe

Black rain-soaked formal coat and skirt suit. Torn sleeve and damaged upper arm continue the split identity without changing costume.
