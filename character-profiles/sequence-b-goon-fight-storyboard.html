Motion Rules

Trailer Cut
  • The batarang throw is inside the melee, not after the fight is done.
  • Batman blocks or redirects one attacker while reaching for the batarang with the other hand.
  • The elevated rifle holder is only threatened, not hit on-screen.
  • The cut must happen before impact so the payoff remains in the full short film.
  • Firearm flashes and optics carry readability; lightning is secondary.
