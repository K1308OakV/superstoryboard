# Character Profiles Motion Design

## Style Prompt

Gritty premium Japanese action-anime production animatic for a rain-soaked Arkham night sequence. The image language is heavy ink, scratch hatching, pitch-black silhouettes, hard cel-shadow blocks, firearm strobe lighting, controlled blue-white optics, red paint accents, wet concrete, smoke, and severe noir contrast.

## Colors

- `#050608` - near-black scene base
- `#d8dde2` - pale sheet / flash contrast
- `#8fd8ff` - night-vision optic blue
- `#d5403a` - red paint and danger accent
- `#c6a86b` - sick institutional lamp warmth

## Typography

- UI and production labels: `Inter`, `Segoe UI`, sans-serif
- Timecode and notes: `Consolas`, `Liberation Mono`, monospace

## What Not To Do

- Do not use clean superhero-poster lighting.
- Do not use bright generic cyberpunk neon.
- Do not make Batman gray or toy-like.
- Do not flatten the female antagonist into glamour art.
- Do not add music, sound effects, or non-female dialogue.
