Nocturne Knight Turnaround - HeyGen V2

First usable studio-sheet direction from HeyGen: four views, readable suit architecture, cape secondary, optics active. This is a review candidate, not final approval.

Turnaround Candidate

What Works

  • Actual sheet: front, three-quarter, side, and rear are present.
  • Contrast: dark armor reads against a pale sheet background.
  • Cape discipline: mantle and cape support the silhouette instead of swallowing it.
  • Mobility: segmented torso, hips, knees, and boots imply movement rather than a heavy statue.

Problems To Fix

  • Crop: feet and outer arms are clipped. Final sheet must show complete body with margin.
  • Generic armor: chest and helmet need a more custom dark-knight identity.
  • Fear silhouette: needs sharper, more severe shoulder and cowl rhythm without copying an existing suit.
  • Weathering: add rain wear, scuffs, and more brutal noir shadow design.

Decision Point

  • Use V2 as the base architecture if the overall body direction is acceptable.
  • Next pass should be a polish pass, not a restart: fix crop, sharpen helmet, reduce generic sci-fi plating, add fear.
  • Do not generate Female Two-Face until this Batman silhouette direction is accepted or corrected.
