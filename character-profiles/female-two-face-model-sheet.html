Final Design Rules

For Fresh Sheet

Identity

Mature copper-blonde woman, late 30s to early 40s, severe eyes, controlled grief, formal black rain-soaked coat and skirt suit, framed photograph held tightly.

Good Half

Keep V3's elegant face, hair mass, and composed expression. The good side must stay human, beautiful, cold, and director-controlled.

Damaged Half

Push the reveal with charred leathery texture, broken scar plates, darker sunken eye shadow, damaged neck and upper arm, and hair loss on the damaged side.

Animation Use

Hide the damaged side in profile first. Reveal it with one hard flicker, lightning edge, muzzle flash, or red emergency bounce. Do not over-explain the origin.
