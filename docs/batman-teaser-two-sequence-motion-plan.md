# Batman Teaser Two-Sequence Motion Plan

## Purpose

This plan starts fresh from the current decision: only two character sheets matter for this scene before motion work:

- Silent armored knight: `character-profiles/assets/heygen-nocturne-knight-v6-gritty-settei.webp`
- Formal split-visage antagonist public/concealed read: `character-profiles/assets/heygen-arkham-widow-split-visage-v2-no-text.webp`
- Formal split-visage antagonist approved identity / good-half anchor: `character-profiles/assets/heygen-arkham-widow-severe-split-burn-v3.webp`
- Formal split-visage antagonist hardest reveal read: `character-profiles/assets/heygen-arkham-widow-charred-half-reveal-v4.webp`
- Formal split-visage antagonist harsher damaged-side material reference: `character-profiles/assets/heygen-arkham-widow-severe-half-char-v5.webp`

The teaser can use two separate sequences because the intervening confrontation/knockback beat is being skipped. The edit does not need continuous geography between the two sections.

## Audio Rule

- No music.
- No sound effects.
- No full dub track.
- Dialogue is allowed only for the female speaker if needed for lip-sync.
- The armored knight remains silent.

## Sequence A - Procession, Duality, Blackout

**Function:** Establish the antagonist, her control, the goons, the frame prop, and the revealed duality.

**Location:** Arkham exterior service courtyard or transfer approach. Rain, wet concrete, institutional walls, ironwork, emergency lamps.

**Visual Beats:**

1. Wide procession: mixed goon group, armed, red paint markings, rain-soaked, moving under weak lamps.
2. Side-profile leader: formal woman holding a framed photograph, one side hidden by hair, frame, and angle.
3. Close hand/frame insert: her grip tightens; rain runs over glass.
4. Goon-light pass: muzzle flash or lamp flicker briefly reveals the marked men.
5. Reveal: angle changes and her damaged side becomes visible in a severe stylized source-material way. Preserve the V3 good-side face as the identity anchor while using V4/V5 only for harsher damaged-side material: absent hair on the damaged side, charred leathery texture, harsh shadow, and torn formalwear continuity, without exposed medical anatomy.
6. Blackout: lights cut, leaving rain, gunmetal silhouettes, and panic.

**Female Dialogue Candidate:**

```text
You made him a symbol.
So I made one too.
```

**Lip-Sync Notes:**

- Only the female antagonist speaks.
- Her mouth movement should be controlled and quiet, not theatrical shouting.
- Dialogue can land over Sequence A and carry into the blackout.

## Sequence B - Night Fight, Optics, Batarang

**Function:** Jump after the skipped beat. No need to explain continuity. The armored knight has entered the fight.

**Location:** Same general Arkham storm environment, but geography may shift to a darker courtyard lane, loading approach, or open service yard.

**Visual Beats:**

1. Hard cut to darkness: rain, muzzle flashes, bodies moving fast, no clean establishing comfort.
2. First combat read: the knight hits from shadow, silent, brutal, economical.
3. Optics close-up: active white-blue slits become the only stable bright point.
4. Goon fire: automatic fire strobes armor plates and cape edges.
5. Upward threat read: a higher-position rifle/laser threat is barely visible.
6. Batarang button: slow-motion throw upward through rain and muzzle-light, Snyder-style time dilation.
7. End frame: the weapon arcs into blackness; cut before impact.

**Motion Rules:**

- Brutal but not gore-focused.
- The knight never speaks.
- Movement should alternate between near-black readability and violent flash-lit clarity.
- Lightning is secondary; firearm flashes and optics carry the visual rhythm.
- Slow motion is reserved for the batarang button, not every hit.

## First Motion Deliverable

Build a silent animatic pass first:

- Character sheet stills
- 8-10 storyboard panels
- 16:9 timeline
- Camera moves and cuts
- Female lip-sync placeholder only where dialogue exists
- No music or sound effects

After the animatic is visually approved, generate paid video clips only for the strongest shots.
