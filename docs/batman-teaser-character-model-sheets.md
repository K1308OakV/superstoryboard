# Batman Teaser Character Model Sheets

## Purpose

Character model sheets come before sequence rendering. This document locks the visual identity, proportions, costume language, emotional read, and render rules for the characters in Sequence 01. The goal is to prevent the generated shots from drifting between frames.

Use this as the studio-style character packet before creating keyframes or clips.

## Global Character Rendering Rules

- Rendering language: premium gritty Japanese action-anime character design, sharp cel shading, hard ink shadows, controlled line weight, cinematic noir contrast.
- Do not copy any specific anime character, scene, costume, or shot.
- Batman should not look like an Attack on Titan character. The anime influence is rendering quality, action intensity, shadow design, and timing language.
- Characters must hold up in darkness, rain, muzzle flashes, and limited lighting.
- Prioritize clear silhouette over decorative detail.
- Avoid overly polished superhero poster poses. These characters belong in a violent Arkham night sequence.

## Sheet A - Batman: Base Armor, Optics Inactive

**Function:** First fear reveal. This is the Batman the goons see before the night-vision close-up.

**Read:** Black armored death-knight silhouette; human but mythic; tactical genius hidden inside a nightmare shape.

**Core Design:**

- Broad armored torso with heavy tactical chest plating.
- Short cowl ears, not long fantasy horns.
- Thick neck and reinforced jaw/cowl structure.
- Cape has mass and weight; it can hide body motion.
- Gauntlets are heavy and practical, with hard angular forearm armor.
- Utility belt is present but subdued.
- Boots are reinforced, grounded, and weighty.
- Armor is black / charcoal with subtle graphite highlights.
- Eye openings exist but are not emphasized.
- No active optic glow in this version.

**Silhouette Rules:**

- Strong shoulders.
- Broad upper body tapering into controlled waist.
- Cape creates a large black triangular mass.
- Head/cowl should read instantly as Batman-inspired without becoming a monster.

**Expression / Performance:**

- No visible mouth expression unless needed.
- The pose should be still, controlled, and predatory.
- He should look like he arrived before anyone noticed.

**Model Sheet Views Needed:**

- Front view.
- Three-quarter view.
- Side view.
- Back/cape mass view.
- Close-up cowl with inactive optics.
- Gauntlet/forearm detail.

**Prompt Anchor:**

```text
studio character model sheet of black armored Batman-inspired vigilante, optics inactive, short cowl ears, broad tactical armor, heavy cape mass, reinforced gauntlets, graphite black armor, futuristic knight silhouette, premium gritty Japanese action anime character design, sharp cel shading, hard ink shadows, clean turnaround poses, white background or neutral dark gray background, no glowing eyes
```

**Negative Prompt:**

```text
glowing eyes, long demon horns, fantasy monster, toy armor, photorealistic cosplay, cute proportions, spandex suit, bright superhero poster, Attack on Titan character, exposed face
```

## Sheet B - Batman: Night-Vision Optics Active

**Function:** Tactical escalation after the blackout and first fight burst.

**Read:** The dark now belongs to Batman.

**Design Differences From Sheet A:**

- Same suit and proportions as inactive version.
- Optics now active with controlled tactical glow.
- The glow should be precise, not magical.
- Background and face framing should be darker and cleaner.
- The optics can suggest night vision / combat targeting, but avoid cluttered HUD elements.

**Close-Up Rules:**

- Cowl fills most of the frame.
- Rain beads on armor.
- Rim light catches cowl edges.
- The optics should be the only major bright element.
- Do not show an activation mechanism in Sequence 01.

**Model Sheet Views Needed:**

- Tight front close-up.
- Three-quarter close-up.
- Low angle close-up.
- Optics detail.

**Prompt Anchor:**

```text
studio close-up model sheet of black armored Batman-inspired vigilante cowl with night-vision optics active, controlled tactical eye glow, rain beads on graphite black armor, short cowl ears, hard jawline cowl, premium gritty Japanese action anime character design, sharp cel shading, hard ink shadows, dark neutral background, backlit rim light, serious tactical presence
```

**Negative Prompt:**

```text
magic glowing eyes, neon superhero poster, visible human eyes, cheerful expression, excessive HUD graphics, cyberpunk helmet unrelated to Batman, toy plastic, photorealistic cosplay
```

## Sheet C - Female Two-Face: Composed Side Concealed Burn

**Function:** First impression in the teaser. She leads the procession before her duality is revealed.

**Read:** Elegant, formal, controlled, dangerous. The audience should sense something withheld.

**Core Design:**

- Blonde, medium shoulder-length hair.
- Late 30s to early 40s.
- Formal attire: tailored coat, suit, or severe formal dress language.
- Clothing should look expensive but rain-soaked and severe.
- Carries a frame object.
- One side of her face is hidden by angle, hair, shadow, or the frame.
- She is attractive and composed, but not glamour-shot soft.

**Color / Texture:**

- Blacks, dark navy, charcoal, muted cream, or severe formal tones.
- Red accent can appear subtly through paint, lining, jewelry, or reflected emergency light.
- Rain and wet fabric matter.

**Performance:**

- Chin level, posture controlled.
- She leads by stillness and authority, not shouting.
- She should feel like grief has hardened into theatrical control.

**Model Sheet Views Needed:**

- Side profile with hidden burned side.
- Three-quarter rear with frame in hand.
- Full-body formal silhouette.
- Hand/frame detail.

**Prompt Anchor:**

```text
studio character model sheet of formal blonde Female Two-Face crime leader, late 30s to early 40s, medium shoulder-length hair, elegant severe formal attire, rain-soaked tailored coat, holding a frame object, one side of face concealed by hair shadow and camera angle, controlled dangerous posture, premium gritty Japanese action anime character design, sharp cel shading, hard ink shadows, noir palette
```

**Negative Prompt:**

```text
full burned side reveal, comedic villain, schoolgirl outfit, oversexualized pose, glamour beauty shoot, gore, smiling pinup, clown costume
```

## Sheet D - Female Two-Face: Burned-Half Reveal

**Function:** Teaser identity reveal. This version is shown in the trailer.

**Read:** Duality, grief, danger, and theatrical control. Not a joke, not a monster gag.

**Design Rules:**

- Same hair, age, outfit, and silhouette as Sheet C.
- Burned half is clearly visible.
- The injury should be readable but not exploitative.
- Do not depict how it happened.
- Do not dramatize the origin.
- The unburned side remains composed and elegant.
- The burned side creates visual asymmetry and emotional tension.

**Performance:**

- She remains composed in the reveal.
- No screaming, no melodrama in the model sheet.
- Her danger comes from control under damage.

**Model Sheet Views Needed:**

- Front reveal.
- Three-quarter reveal.
- Lighting test: half her face in flicker / lightning / muzzle flash.
- Expression sheet: calm, cold anger, near-smile, silent grief.

**Prompt Anchor:**


## Sheet E - Painted Goon Lineup

**Function:** Establish the crew before the fight and provide readable opponents for Batman.

**Read:** A diverse criminal unit with a shared visual identity. They should feel dangerous and organized, not random.

**Representation Rules:**

- Include white, Black, Hispanic, Indian/South Asian, and other plausible American urban backgrounds.
- Majority can be white, but representation must be visible.
- Do not assign cowardice, stupidity, brutality, or leadership based on ethnicity.
- Vary age, body type, hair, clothes, and weapon handling naturally.

**Shared Design:**

- Red trail or stripe on one side of face/clothing for some.
- Half-and-half paint for others.
- Tactical street clothing, rain jackets, workwear, security gear, or stolen institutional pieces.
- Firearms: handguns, rifles, automatic weapons.
- Paint should echo Female Two-Face's duality without becoming clownish.

**Lineup Count For Sheet:**

- 8 goons total.
- 4 white goons with varied ages/body types.
- 1 Black goon.
- 1 Hispanic goon.
- 1 Indian/South Asian goon.
- 1 additional mixed/ambiguous background goon.

**Model Sheet Views Needed:**

- Full crew lineup.
- Three weapon poses.
- Paint marking close-ups.
- Silhouette test in muzzle flash.

**Prompt Anchor:**

```text
studio character lineup sheet of eight diverse painted criminal goons for rain-soaked Arkham action scene, majority white with Black Hispanic Indian South Asian and mixed-background members represented respectfully, shared red stripe and half-face paint markings, tactical street clothing and rain gear, firearms held safely in model poses, varied ages body types and hair, gritty noir criminal crew, premium Japanese action anime character design, sharp cel shading, hard ink shadows, neutral model sheet background
```

**Negative Prompt:**

```text
ethnic caricature, comedy gang, clown costumes, identical clones, gore, Nazi imagery, extremist symbols, bright daylight, cute mascot design, oversexualized characters
```

## Sheet F - Elevated Rifle Threat

**Function:** The above threat Batman identifies after night vision activates.

**Read:** A dangerous presence above, barely visible to everyone else, readable to Batman.

**Design Options:**

- Could be a sniper-like rifle holder on an Arkham catwalk.
- Could be one of Female Two-Face's hidden assets.
- Keep identity partially obscured for teaser mystery.

**Visual Rules:**

- Thin red laser, not a giant beam.
- Rain catches the laser subtly.
- Figure is mostly silhouette.
- Weapon and posture are readable.
- Elevated location: catwalk, tower, upper window, or security platform.

**Model Sheet Views Needed:**

- Silhouette with rifle.
- Laser-in-rain lighting test.
- Elevated platform thumbnail.

**Prompt Anchor:**

```text
studio silhouette model sheet of elevated rifle threat on Arkham catwalk at night, figure mostly hidden in rain and darkness, thin red laser sight visible through rain haze, tactical rifle posture, noir atmosphere, premium gritty Japanese action anime design, sharp cel shading, hard ink shadows, minimal detail for teaser mystery
```

**Negative Prompt:**

```text
giant laser beam, bright clear face reveal, sci-fi space soldier, comedy pose, overexposed lighting, gore
```

## Character Render Order

Render character models before story shots:

1. Batman inactive optics turnaround.
2. Batman active optics close-up sheet.
3. Female Two-Face concealed side sheet.
4. Female Two-Face burned-half reveal sheet.
5. Painted goon lineup.
6. Elevated rifle threat silhouette.

After these are approved, render the Sequence 01 keyframes.

## Approval Checklist

- Batman silhouette holds across inactive and active optics.
- Female Two-Face is recognizable as the same person before and after reveal.
- Burned-half design is clear, serious, and non-exploitative.
- Goon lineup has visible diversity without caricature.
- Paint language ties the crew to Female Two-Face.
- All designs can survive darkness, rain, muzzle flashes, and fast editing.
