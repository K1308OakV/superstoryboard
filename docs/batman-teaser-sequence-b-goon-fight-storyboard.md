# Sequence B - Goon Fight / Mid-Melee Batarang Storyboard

## Purpose

This storyboard covers only the second teaser half: Batman is already in the fight with the goons. The skipped confrontation with Female Two-Face is not shown here. The batarang throw is not a final victory beat; it happens while Batman is still surrounded and actively fighting.

The teaser cuts before the batarang reaches the elevated rifle threat. Impact and consequence belong to the full short film, not this trailer section.

## Core Rules

- Batman remains silent.
- The fight is brutal, efficient, and tactical, but not gore-focused.
- Batman is outnumbered throughout the section.
- The batarang throw is tactical multitasking inside the melee.
- Muzzle flashes are the main readable light source.
- Lightning is used sparingly.
- The night-vision optics activate during combat, not before it.
- The elevated rifle/laser threat is seen only enough for the audience to understand Batman noticed it.
- The final teaser button is the batarang rotating upward through rain in slow motion, cutting before impact.

## Location

Arkham exterior service yard / courtyard lane at night. Wet concrete, rain, institutional walls, security catwalk or upper window line above the ground fight.

## Animation Notes

- Use quick, readable silhouettes instead of continuous full-light choreography.
- Keep Batman in frame fragments: shoulder, cape edge, gauntlet, cowl, boots, then full silhouette.
- Muzzle flashes should expose action for one beat, then collapse back into darkness.
- The optics activation should be the only calm beat in the chaos.
- The batarang should rotate visibly, with rain streaks crossing it in slow motion.
- Do not show the elevated rifle holder being hit.
